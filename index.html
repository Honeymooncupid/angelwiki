Angel Geoff Dare (born February 9, 2006) is a Filipino information technology student, guitarist, watercolor painter and home cook[1]. Born in Angeles City and based in the City of San Fernando, both in Pampanga, he is currently in the second year of the Bachelor of Science in Information Technology program at the City College of San Fernando[2].

Early life

Dare was born on February 9, 2006, in Angeles City, Pampanga. He later settled in the City of San Fernando, the provincial capital, where he lives today[1].

Education

Dare is a second-year student of the BS Information Technology program at the City College of San Fernando, Pampanga[2]. His studies cover computing and software, and he is expected to graduate after completing the full four-year curriculum.[citation needed]

Interests and hobbies

Guitar

Playing guitar is among Dare’s long-standing pastimes. It serves as a creative outlet alongside his technical studies.

Watercolor painting

Dare paints in watercolor, a medium known for its fluid, translucent washes of color.

Cooking

Cooking is his third main pastime. Critics (mostly family) have reportedly described the results as “worth waiting for.”[who?]

Personal life and health

Dare has been diagnosed with bipolar I disorder, a mood condition involving episodes of elevated and low mood. He lives with it while studying and keeping up his creative pursuits[1]. Music, painting and cooking are among the activities he enjoys outside of school.

Dare shares this about himself voluntarily. Bipolar disorder is a common, manageable condition, and many people with it study, work and create.
